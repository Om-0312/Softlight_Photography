About Bharat Kamble

Behind the Lens

Hello! I’m Bharat Kamble, the founder of Softlight Photography. Based in Chh.Sambhajinagar, I specialize in wedding, maternity, and creative storytelling photography. My goal is simple: to turn your moments into timeless memories. Whether it's a joyful wedding or an intimate maternity shoot, I strive to capture raw emotion, light, and detail in every frame.

With years of experience and a passion for people and stories, Softlight Photography ensures each project feels personal, beautiful, and unforgettable.

  • 5+ years of professional photography experience
  • Specializes in weddings, maternity, and pre-wedding shoots
  • Based in Chh.Sambhajinagar, serving clients across the region
  • Combines technical skill with a creative eye for detail
  • Known for candid and creative storytelling
